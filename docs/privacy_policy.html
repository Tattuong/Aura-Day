Privacy Policy – Aura Day

Aura Day is a private, on-device life dashboard. Habits, check-ins, tasks, goals, focus reminders, coins, and shop unlocks stay on this phone. We do not create an account.

Data stored on the device

  • Habits, check-ins, tasks, and goals
  • Reminder times, scheduled on this phone
  • Coin balance and shop unlocks
  • Purchase tokens needed to restore Remove ads and Aura Day Pro

Services

  • Google Play Billing – coin packs, Remove ads, and Aura Day Pro (handled by Google)
  • Remote config – GET https://api2.blwsmartware.net/N319.json to read the disable field (no personal data is sent)

Contact the developer through the Play Store listing.
