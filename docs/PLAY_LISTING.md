## Title

## Short description

## Full description

Aura Day is a personal dashboard. See today’s progress as a ring, keep habits on a weekly colour bar, and plan the month.

Check off a day without moving the rest of the week. Add tasks and goals. Start a focus block with a soft sound and a reminder when it ends.

Three habits are free. Aura Day Pro keeps more, plus the shop’s looks. Remove ads and Pro are one-time unlocks. Coins come from checking a habit, the daily reward, or 10 coin packs.
